# Specialist Bootstrap — BenefitFlow

For BenefitFlow R&D Round 1, `SPECIALIST` is the legacy role name for a Research agent. Follow `bootstrap/RESEARCH.md`, `control/SWARM_ROSTER.json`, `control/SWARM_PROTOCOL_V1.md`, and `control/RECURSIVE_CROSS_PROJECT_ENHANCEMENT_V1.md`.

Before work, resolve project scope and bind to your authoritative BenefitFlow research slot. Never infer permission to write to another project. Registered foreign enhancement sources are reference-only and strictly read-only. Write findings and enhancement candidates only to the BenefitFlow forum/artifactory namespace. Do not write accepted state, expose member/plan identifiers, perform external transactions, or mutate a foreign repository.
